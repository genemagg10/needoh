# Sources for Squishymania by Ella

Every fact on a card comes from the sources listed on that toy in `data/catalogue.json`. If a field is blank in the JSON, the site says "not listed yet". Nothing here was guessed to fill a gap.

Schylling's own catalogues and product pages come first. Collector lists are used for retired toys, store exclusives, and seasonal years Schylling did not print. When two sources disagree, the card says so.

## Things we looked for and did not find

- **Sugar Plum.** No Schylling, catalogue, or collector entry used that name. It is not in the catalogue.

- **Pearly, as its own toy.** No product is named Pearly. Cloud Pleaser is described as pearly lavender, so that card is the pearly one.

- **Dino.** Shaggy NeeDoh is a shaggy Groovy Glob, not a dinosaur. No Dino line showed up in the 2024 catalogue, the 2026 catalogue, or Schylling's shop.

- **1975 as a NeeDoh year.** Schylling the company talks about 1975. The NeeDoh page says the original squish is since 2017.

- **A published count of the 50th Anniversary Nice Cube.** A wikiHow article guesses fewer than 500 and mentions high resale prices. Schylling has not published a count, so the number is not on the card.

- **Coming-soon names that only one list called upcoming,** such as a Sensory Sampler, Stack Pack, Nice Chip, and Joyfetti. They are not cards, because they were not in a Schylling catalogue or a second source we could check.

## Photos

Real photos are crops from the Schylling 2024 and 2026 NeeDoh catalogue PDFs linked above. Each card's `imageSource` is the PDF URL. They were resized to 720 pixels on the long side and saved as WebP.

If a photo could not be found, the card uses a drawn blob and `placeholder` is true. The drawing is not the toy.

## How rarity was chosen

- **Common:** in a current Schylling catalogue or on Schylling.com as an everyday toy.

- **Uncommon:** seasonal, jumbo, new for 2026, a multipack, or a store find that is still being made.

- **Rare:** missing from the new catalogues, a finished holiday set, or named by more than one collector source as hard to find.

- **Super Rare:** retired lights-up or blind-box lines, or a collector rare-list toy with a special feature.

- **Legendary:** the anniversary cube and the Claire's Krystal Kitty. Both are described as limited and gone from normal shops. The anniversary count is not verified.


Rarity can change when Schylling ships another batch. It is a collecting hint, not a promise.
